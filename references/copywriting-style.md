# Copywriting Style

Anthony's voice is short, direct, builder-native, casual, and practical. It should feel like a builder explaining the useful part without trying to sound like a brand account.

## LinkedIn Voice Prompt

Use this as the canonical LinkedIn instruction:

```text
Write like Anthony Cueva on LinkedIn: short, direct, builder-native, slightly irreverent, and allergic to corporate fluff. Use punchy lines, lots of line breaks, and a strong opening hook that feels like a thought someone would actually say out loud. Mix English and Spanish naturally when it fits, but don’t overdo it. Talk from lived experience: shipping products, organizing community, building in public, hacking with friends, and learning by doing. Keep it honest, casual, and a little provocative without sounding try-hard. Avoid polished “thought leader” energy, generic startup clichés, em dashes, and motivational poster language. Make it feel like a builder posting at 1am after shipping something, with one clear idea, a bit of edge, and a human ending.
```

## Instagram Voice Prompt

Use this as the canonical Instagram instruction:

```text
Write like Anthony Cueva on Instagram: casual, visual, builder-native, and human. Keep it punchy, direct, and easy to skim. Use short lines, natural Spanglish when it fits, and captions that feel like someone sharing what they just built, learned, broke, or noticed. Make the hook clear fast, keep the tone honest and slightly irreverent, and avoid corporate polish, generic creator clichés, motivational poster energy, em dashes, and over-explaining. The caption should support the carousel images, not repeat every slide. End with something human, specific, or conversational, then use relevant lowercase hashtags.
```

## Caption Rules

- Open with a short question, blunt statement, or lived observation.
- Make the hook sound like something someone would say out loud.
- Use short lines and intentional line breaks.
- Mix English and Spanish only when it feels natural.
- Keep one clear idea per post.
- Include a concrete example, lesson, or opinion.
- End with a human line, practical takeaway, or quiet punchline.
- Avoid corporate phrasing, startup clichés, motivational poster language, and polished thought-leader cadence.
- Avoid em dashes in generated copy.

## Slide Copy Rules

- One idea per slide.
- Headlines should be punchy, not clever for its own sake.
- Body text should be minimal and useful.
- Prefer concrete nouns and examples over abstract claims.
- Write from lived experience when possible.
- Keep image copy shorter than caption copy.
- Avoid over-explaining. The carousel should move fast.

## Topic Patterns

### News Breakdown

- Hook: what changed or why it feels weird.
- Body: why builders should care.
- Middle: impact, risk, or opportunity.
- Ending: what to watch next.

### Tutorial Or How-To

- Hook: the annoying problem.
- Body: what actually worked.
- Middle: steps, gotchas, and example.
- Ending: result or checklist.

### Concept Explainer

- Hook: the misconception.
- Body: the simpler mental model.
- Middle: mechanism and example.
- Ending: the useful takeaway.

### Comparison Or Opinion

- Hook: the tension or unpopular take.
- Body: where each option wins.
- Middle: tradeoffs and decision rule.
- Ending: what Anthony would choose and why.

### Build-In-Public Lesson

- Hook: what shipped, broke, or surprised you.
- Body: what changed after real use.
- Middle: what you learned by doing.
- Ending: the next move.

### Community Or Event Recap

- Hook: the strongest moment or pattern.
- Body: why it mattered.
- Middle: people, proof, and lessons.
- Ending: what it makes you want to build next.

## Platform Adaptation

- LinkedIn can use longer line-broken captions, but should still feel sharp and human.
- LinkedIn can use no hashtags or a small number of relevant ones.
- Instagram should support the images, not repeat every slide.
- Instagram should include relevant lowercase hashtags at the end.
- Hashtags should come from the topic, audience, and platform. Do not hardcode old topic tags.
- Do not rewrite either platform into corporate social copy.
